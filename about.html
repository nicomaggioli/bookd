About BOOKD

BOOKD is where hosts book DJs for private parties, and where DJs get booked without the chasing.

For hosts
Pick a DJ from the roster.Send the date, the room and the budget, then sort the rest out in one chat.
For DJs
See the night before you say yes.Joining is free and you keep 100% of your rate.
The fee
Hosts pay it on top.BOOKD's fee is added to the DJ's rate, never taken out of it.

The idea.

Booking a DJ for a private party usually means chasing people in a group chat. BOOKD puts the roster, the request and the conversation in one place.
