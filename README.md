# BOOKD

DJs for private parties. This is the public site.

- `index.html` is the homepage
- `roster.html` is the roster and `artist.html` is the profile page (one page for every artist, picked by the address, like `artist.html#kozlow`)
- `artists.html` is the page for DJs who want to join
- `login.html` is log in and sign up. Accounts are not open yet and the page says so
- `terms.html` is terms and privacy, `contact.html` is the contact form, and `404.html` is the page not found
- `assets/` holds the photos and videos
- `bookd-config.js`, `bookd-api.js` and `bookd-supabase.js` are the connection to the backend. The two keys in `bookd-config.js` are empty, so nothing is saved or sent yet

The logged in screens (inbox, requests, dashboards) are still designs and are not part of this site yet.
