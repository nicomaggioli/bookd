# BOOKD

DJs for private parties. The whole v6 site as a clickable design: hosts, DJs and the admin dashboard.

- `index.html` is the homepage
- `roster.html` and `kaytran.html` are the roster and a DJ profile
- `inbox.html`, `inbox-new.html`, `booking.html`, `cancel.html` and `account.html` are the host side
- `artists.html`, `login.html`, `build.html`, `dashboard.html` and `requests.html` are the DJ side
- `admin.html` is the admin dashboard, `messages.html` shows the emails and texts, `terms.html` is terms and privacy, and `404.html` is the page not found
- `assets/` holds the photos and videos

Dates, prices and placeholders in brackets are samples. Nothing is connected to a backend yet.
