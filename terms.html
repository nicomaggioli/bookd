Terms.

Last updated November 14, 2026. The deal between you and BOOKD LLC when you use BOOKD, whether you're throwing the party or playing it.

1Who we are

BOOKD is run by BOOKD LLC, a limited liability company registered in New York, with its mailing address at P.O. Box 2214, Brooklyn, NY 11202. When this page says "we" or "us", that's who it means.

2What BOOKD does

BOOKD is where hosts find DJs for private parties, hear their sets and book them in one chat. Our part in each booking is to connect you, carry the chat and handle the money. The booking itself is between the host and the DJ. Some DJs have an agent or manager who chats, sends offers and signs for them. When they do, that person speaks for the DJ, but it's still the DJ who plays your party. We don't play the set.

Who's in town and when comes from the DJs themselves. We check what we can, but we can't promise every date is right.

3Your account

You need to be 18 or older to make an account. Use an email you actually check. DJs can use their artist name.

Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for everything that happens under your account. You can close your account any time from your account page, and we can suspend or close accounts that break these terms, with a heads up when we can.

4Booking a DJ

Here's how it works. You send a request with the date, the room, your budget and the vibe. The DJ, or their team, replies in the chat with an offer: a price, set times, travel and their rider. You accept, agree to the contract, pay the deposit, and the date is locked.

That's the moment the offer becomes binding. It's still a deal between the host and the DJ, even when their agent or manager made it. BOOKD sets it up and holds the money.

5Payments and fees

Hosts pay a deposit of 50% when they accept an offer, and the rest within 48 hours after the party. The host pays a 7% booking fee on top of the DJ's rate. That's what BOOKD keeps, and the DJ gets 100% of their rate. Payments are handled by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax, where it applies, is shown before you pay.

DJs get paid 5 days after the party.

6Cancellations and refunds

Plans change. Here's what happens when they do.

The host cancels
14 days or more before the party, the whole deposit comes back. 7 to 13 days, half of it does. Inside 7 days, the deposit goes to the DJ for holding the date. In every case, you don't owe the balance.
The DJ cancels
The host gets every dollar back, booking fee included, and the roster shows them who else is free that night, so they can pick someone new. A DJ who cancels twice in a year comes off the roster.
Nobody's fault
Weather, a venue closing, that kind of thing: the booking is called off, the host gets the whole deposit back, and nobody owes anyone anything.
Refunds
Back to the card you paid with, within 5 to 10 business days.

7For DJs on the roster

Joining the roster is free, and you see the date, the room and the budget before you say yes to any request. Your relationship with BOOKD LLC is that of an independent contractor. You're not our employee, and you set your own fees and dates.

Keep your dates honest. If you say you're in a city that weekend, hosts plan their party around it. Show up, play the set you agreed to, and keep it respectful in the room.

8House rules

Most of this is common sense. Be kind to the people you book or play for. Tell the truth in requests and on profiles. Don't ask the DJ to plug in your phone.

What's not allowed: harassment, fake profiles or requests, paying outside BOOKD for a booking you found here, and anything illegal. Breaking these can mean a warning, a cancelled booking or losing your account.

9When things go wrong

Parties are live, and live things are unpredictable. Here's who's responsible for what.

BOOKD
Keeping the site running, passing your messages, holding the money safely and paying it out on time.
Hosts
The venue, the guests, any permits, and a safe, dry spot for the booth.
DJs
Showing up on time and playing the set they agreed to. Who brings the gear is agreed between the DJ and the host before the night.
The most we'd owe
What you paid BOOKD in fees over the last 12 months, or $100, whichever is more.
What we don't promise
BOOKD comes as is. We can't promise every DJ, date or party goes perfectly, but we'll always try to make it right.
Your rights by law
Nothing here takes away rights you have by law that a contract can't change.

10Disputes and the law

If a host and a DJ don't agree on something, start in the chat. If that doesn't fix it, write to hello@bookdplatform.com. We read the chat and the booking, talk to both sides and make a call within 10 business days.

These terms are governed by the laws of New York. Disputes go to the state or federal courts in Kings County, New York, or to small claims court if they qualify.

11Changes to these terms

BOOKD will change as it grows, and so will this page. If we change these terms, we'll email you at least 14 days before the change kicks in. The date at the top shows when they last changed.

Privacy.

Last updated November 14, 2026. What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. BOOKD LLC looks after your data under US privacy law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies.

1What we collect

Here's what BOOKD collects when you use it:

  • The name, email and password you make your account with.
  • Your phone number, so your DJ can find the door and we can text you about your booking.
  • What you put in a request: the date, the room, your budget and the vibe.
  • Your messages with DJs or hosts.
  • If you're a DJ: your artist name, home city, the cities and dates you add, your sets, your fee range and your payout details, which Stripe keeps for us.
  • Payment details, handled by Stripe. We never see your full card number.
  • Your location, if you tap Use my location.
  • Basic usage and device data, like your browser, your device type and the pages you visit.

That's the complete list.

2How we use it

To run BOOKD: showing you DJs who are in town, passing messages between hosts and DJs, taking payments and keeping you posted about your booking. Beyond that, we use it to make BOOKD better and keep it safe.

We use it because you asked us to set up a booking, and because you agreed to these terms. Marketing emails only go out if you opt in, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

3Who sees it

When you send a request, the DJ and their team see your name, the details of the night and your messages. When a DJ sends an offer, you see theirs. Once it's bookd, your DJ also gets your phone number.

If you're a DJ, hosts see your profile: your name, your sound, your sets and the cities and dates you add. Your fee range isn't shown on your profile.

We also use other companies to help run the site: Stripe for payments, Supabase for accounts and data, Vercel for hosting, Twilio for texts and Resend for email. Each one gets only what it needs to do its job. We don't sell your data, and we won't.

4How long we keep it

Account details
As long as your account is open.
Booking records
7 years, because tax law says so.
Chats
2 years after your last booking.
After that
We delete it, or strip out anything that says who you are.

5Cookies

Sadly not the snack kind. BOOKD uses two: one keeps you logged in and one remembers your settings. No ad trackers. You can block them in your browser, but then you'll have to log in every time.

6Your choices

To ask about your data, write to hello@bookdplatform.com. You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to fix it or ask us to delete it. We reply within 30 days.

Not happy with the answer? You can complain to your state's attorney general.

7Kids

BOOKD is for grown-ups planning parties. You need to be 18 or older to use it. If someone younger signs up, we close the account and delete their data.

8Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted, and only the people who need it can see it. If something goes wrong, we'll email you within 72 hours of finding out.

On your side, pick a password you don't use anywhere else.

9Talk to us

Questions about this page, or anything else? Write to hello@bookdplatform.com or P.O. Box 2214, Brooklyn, NY 11202. A real person on the team reads every one.
