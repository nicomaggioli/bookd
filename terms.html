Privacy.

Last updated [Date]. What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. [Company name] looks after your data under [Data protection law].

1What we collect

Here's what BOOKD collects when you use it:

  • The name, email and password you sign up with.
  • Your phone number, so your DJ can find the door and we can text you about your booking.
  • What you put in a request: the date, the room, your budget and the vibe.
  • Your messages with DJs or hosts.
  • If you're a DJ: your artist name, home city, the cities and dates you add, your sets, your fee range and [Payout details].
  • Payment details, handled by [Payment processor].
  • Your location, if you tap Use my location.
  • [Usage and device data]

The complete list is [Data we keep].

2How we use it

To run BOOKD: showing you DJs who are in town, passing messages between hosts and DJs, taking payments and keeping you posted about your booking. Anything else: [Other uses].

Our legal basis for this is [Legal basis]. Marketing emails: [Marketing email policy].

3Who sees it

When you send a request, the DJ and their team see your name, the details of the night and your messages. When a DJ sends an offer, you see theirs. Once it's booked, your DJ also gets your phone number.

If you're a DJ, hosts see your profile: your name, your sound, your sets, your fee range and the cities and dates you add.
